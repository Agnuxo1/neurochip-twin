AI for neural organ-on-a-chip · developmental neurotoxicity

Measure a few concentrations. Forecast the rest.

NeuroChip Twin predicts how a chemical it has never seen reshapes the development of a neural network, across the full dose range and developmental time, from only a few measured concentrations. Every forecast comes with a 90 % band whose coverage is calibrated out-of-fold, and the model flags when it does not know.

Evidence boundaries

  • Biology: rat primary cortical cultures on 48-well multi-electrode array plates (US EPA Network Formation Assay). This is not a validated twin of any commercial organ-on-a-chip.
  • Chip layer: propagation parameters come from real Brewer 4-compartment hippocampal MEA recordings; any compartment "what-if" scenario built on them is a simulation, not a measurement.
  • Not clinical: forecasts are research outputs for screening design. They are not safety, regulatory or medical decisions.
  • Held-out means held-out: each chemical is scored with the ensemble of its own cross-validation fold, which never saw any of its wells. The explorer uses one evenly spread design per k (with k = 1 the model sees only the lowest concentration); the headline numbers average several seeded random designs per k.
  • Bands: the explorer draws the ensemble's parametric 90 % band; the conformal calibration reported above is evaluated out-of-fold in the paper pipeline.
